Support

Everwhile makes time in the apps you choose easier to notice with neutral reminders. Today shows total time, hourly usage and time by app on this iPhone.

  1. Allow Screen Time access, then choose one or more apps.
  2. Choose a reminder interval and start monitoring. Allow notifications for reminders.
  3. Open Today to see usage so far today. Hourly totals are not exact app opening or closing times.

You can replay Quick Start in About & Support. iOS may delay reminders and report updates; alert presentation is controlled by iPhone notification settings.

For help or feedback: zhangs.taq@gmail.com. Select the address to copy it. Please do not send sensitive Screen Time screenshots unless necessary.

支持

Everwhile 通过中性提醒，让你留意所选 App 中流逝的时间。Today 在这台 iPhone 上显示今日总量、每小时分布与各 App 用时。

  1. 授权屏幕使用时间，然后选择一个或多个 App。
  2. 设置提醒间隔并开始监控；允许通知以接收提醒。
  3. 打开 Today 查看今天截至现在的使用情况。小时汇总不是 App 精确打开或关闭时间。

可在“关于与支持”中重播新手教学。提醒与报告更新可能延迟，通知显示方式由 iPhone 设置管理。

帮助或反馈：zhangs.taq@gmail.com。选中邮箱即可复制。没有必要时，请勿发送敏感的屏幕使用时间截图。
