Privacy Policy

Everwhile processes Screen Time data on your device through Apple’s Screen Time APIs. Everwhile does not upload Screen Time usage data to an Everwhile backend.

Your app selections and reminder settings are stored locally. Usage reports remain within the on-device report extension. The app and monitor share only local configuration and reminder lifecycle records, not report usage data.

Everwhile has no account system, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. These pages contain no tracking scripts or cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary web requests under its own policies.

If you choose to contact support, your email provider sends your message and the support mailbox receives the information you include. This is optional and separate from automatic app processing. Avoid including private app identities or usage unless needed. Contact support to ask about removing a message you sent.

隐私政策

Everwhile 通过 Apple 的屏幕使用时间 API，在你的设备上处理屏幕使用时间数据。Everwhile 不会将使用数据上传至 Everwhile 后端。

App 选择和提醒设置保存在本机。使用报告数据留在设备上的报告扩展内。主 App 与监控扩展仅共享本地配置和提醒生命周期记录，不共享报告使用数据。

Everwhile 没有账号系统、广告 SDK 或分析 SDK。这些网页没有跟踪脚本或 Cookie；托管提供方可能依据其自身政策处理普通网页请求。

若你主动联系支持，邮件由你的邮件服务提供方发送，支持邮箱会收到你填写的信息。这是可选操作，与 App 自动处理数据分开。没有必要时请勿包含私人 App 名称或用时；如需删除你发送的邮件，可联系支持。
