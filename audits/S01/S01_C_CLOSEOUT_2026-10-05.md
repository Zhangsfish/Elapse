## Natural-midnight acceptance

The 47.1 implementation was not deliberately kept running until another natural midnight solely to observe `generation N -> N+1`.

Historical 44.1 natural-midnight evidence already established the important negative fact: system registration and full event-count readback can remain present across midnight even when the old nonrepeating schedule did not prove a new day's interval was active.

S01-B subsequently added:
- daily `repeats=true`;
- intervalDidStart generation + anchor;
- same-cycle idempotence;
- new-cycle interval-local receipt reset;
- unit coverage for generation rollover;
- fail-closed behavior when no current interval anchor exists.

The owner explicitly chose not to spend another night on duplicate acceptance testing. The remaining 47.1 natural-midnight observation is therefore accepted as a **residual runtime risk**, not silently marked as device-proven.

If a real future midnight exposes a problem, reopen the lifecycle issue then.

## Residual boundaries carried forward

Not blocking S02:
- 47.1 natural-midnight `generation N -> N+1` not directly observed;
- timezone/DST not manually manipulated on device;
- all-day delivery of every DeviceActivity threshold is not proven.

These are reliability notes, not UI requirements.

## Decision

**S01 COMPLETE — PASS_WITH_NOTES.**

The reliability foundation is sufficient to move to S02. Do not mix the residual calendar note into ordinary S02 UI work unless a real regression appears.
