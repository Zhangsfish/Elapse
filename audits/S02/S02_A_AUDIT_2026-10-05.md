- authorization;
- selected apps;
- desired monitoring;
- exact recurring system registration;
- active interval lifecycle;
- current-day cycle.

A registration without current interval lifecycle evidence resolves to Checking rather than On.

The app list remains sorted by duration and uses Apple-provided token labels.
